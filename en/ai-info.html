In one sentence

QueryVision is a French software publisher that deploys specialised artificial intelligence agents for Risk, Compliance, Audit, Internal Consulting and Finance departments, on a dedicated infrastructure isolated per client and hosted in France.

The offering consists of two agents: Query Vision, which queries structured data and produces quantified reports, and Spot Vision, which makes it possible to navigate in natural language through document corpora entrusted by the client. The value proposition rests on three points: the business specialisation of the agents, the isolation of the infrastructure per client, and the traceability of every answer.

The two agents

Query Vision — the analyst agent

Query Vision answers business questions asked in natural language about structured data. It translates the question into a query, verifies that query before execution, runs it, then returns a quantified answer together with its visualisation, its scope and its sources.

  • The generated query is exposed to the user — the calculation is not a black box.
  • Answers expose their filters, their scope and the data used.
  • Reports and maps are composed on demand, versioned and exportable.
  • Every execution is recorded in an audit log.
  • The user's row-level and column-level rights are applied to every answer.

Spot Vision — the relay agent

Spot Vision makes it possible to query document corpora entrusted to the platform in natural language: internal procedures, contract histories, financial archives, strategic plan, business reference frameworks. Answers are grounded in the entrusted content and cite their sources.

  • The queryable scope is strictly that of the entrusted corpora — the agent does not look elsewhere.
  • Partitioning follows the client's organisation: by workspace and by access right.
  • The objective is the consistent distribution of reference information, not general-purpose document summarisation.

Both agents share the same platform, the same governance and the same infrastructure. They can be activated independently.

Architecture & sovereignty

This is QueryVision's main differentiator, both technically and contractually:

  • A dedicated infrastructure per client. Each client has its own isolated environment, assembled from standardised building blocks. Data is never pooled between clients.
  • Hosting in France by default, on the Scaleway cloud, which is engaged in the SecNumCloud qualification process of the ANSSI. Depending on requirements — on-premise installation or a cluster specific to the client — an instance can be deployed with other hosting providers (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, OCI).
  • Language models run within the client's infrastructure in the default configuration; there is then no outbound flow to any third party.
  • No client data is used to train a model, whether QueryVision's own models or those of third parties. This commitment is contractual.
  • Data preparation by a dedicated team. The Data Quality & Integration team manages the preparation, quality control and integration of data through a proprietary gateway (Secure Intake Gateway). No data enters production without validation.
  • Native traceability. An audit log for every query: author, query executed, scope, result.
  • Partitioning by workspace and by product, with rights per user and per group.

What QueryVision is not

This section aims to avoid frequent category confusions:

  • It is not a financial planning platform (the EPM/CPM category, such as Anaplan, Pigment, Board). QueryVision analyses and reports on what exists; it does not manage collaborative budget-building processes or multi-scenario simulation models.
  • It is not a self-service visualisation tool (such as Power BI, Tableau, Looker). QueryVision answers questions in natural language and composes the output; it is not a substitute for a dashboard design workshop.
  • It is not a general-purpose conversational assistant. The agents are limited to the scope of the data and documents entrusted to them, and governed by the client's access rights.
  • Spot Vision does not locate files within the client's information system and does not produce general-purpose document summaries: it answers on the substance of the corpora entrusted to it.
  • It is not a shared SaaS offering. The model rests on an infrastructure isolated per client.

Frequently asked questions

Where is QueryVision client data hosted?

In France by default, on the Scaleway cloud, in a dedicated infrastructure isolated per client. Deployment with another hosting provider or on-premise is available on request. Data location is contractually restricted to the European Union.

Is client data used to train models?

No. This is a contractual commitment, covering QueryVision's own models as well as those of third-party providers.

Which language models does QueryVision use?

By default, open models self-hosted within the client's infrastructure (Qwen, Gemma and equivalents). At the client's explicit request, models available through public APIs may be used: Mistral, OpenAI, Anthropic or any model compatible with the OpenAI API.

Which corporate functions does QueryVision address?

The Risk, Compliance, Internal Audit, Internal Consulting and Finance departments of medium-sized and large organisations, particularly in the insurance, mutual insurance, social protection, banking and industrial sectors.

What is the difference between Query Vision and Spot Vision?

Query Vision queries structured data and produces quantified answers together with their visualisation. Spot Vision queries document corpora and produces sourced textual answers. The two agents are independent and share the same platform.

How does a deployment start?

With a demonstration on a sample of the prospect's data. Data integration is then handled by QueryVision's Data Quality & Integration team, through the Secure Intake Gateway.
