01 — Platform overview

One platform, one vendor, one infrastructure.

QueryVision is a SaaS platform for data analysis and decision intelligence, embedding LLM, NLP and document-processing modules to automate analyses, summaries and decision-support tasks.

It is operated as an exclusive SaaS: QueryVision relies on no service providers or integrators to deliver the platform. All source code, algorithms and documentation belong to QueryVision.

02 — Hosting & sovereignty

Sovereign by default, not by option.

Every component of the platform — frontend, backend, SQL databases and object storage — is hosted at Scaleway S.A.S., a French sovereign cloud provider certified ISO/IEC 27001 and HDS, located in France.

By default, LLM models are hosted and executed locally on our Scaleway machines — inference never leaves our sovereign infrastructure. No client data transits through any third party for AI processing in the standard configuration.

The Mistral ZDR (Zero Data Retention) option is available for clients who wish to access Mistral models via API with a contractual zero-retention guarantee: no data is retained or used for training by Mistral AI.

Commitments — Security, AI & incidents

Governed, controlled, documented.

Our technical and organizational measures are formalized in our Security Assurance Plan (SAP), available to qualified clients and prospects on request.

03 — Security & access control

Security owned directly by the CTO.

It covers authentication and permission management (SSO, MFA, RBAC), encryption of data in transit and at rest, environment isolation, and continuous audit and traceability. The full detail of the measures (authentication, encryption, audit) is available through our Security Assurance Plan.

04 — Sub-processors & cloud partners

A limited number of sub-processors, all located in the EU.

Hosting and AI inference are entrusted to sub-processors bound by strict contractual confidentiality and security clauses. Any change is notified to the affected clients before it takes effect. The exhaustive register (roles, purposes, certifications) and the DPA are available on request.

06 — Responsible AI

LLMs integrated under strict principles.

Sovereign inference by default (Scaleway EU infrastructure), Zero Data Retention option, exclusive client ownership of data, no automated decision without human oversight. These commitments (inference architecture, ZDR options, data governance) are formalized in our Security Assurance Plan.

07 — Incidents & vulnerabilities

A formalized process, aligned with GDPR and ANSSI.

Client notification timelines, the classification matrix (impact/urgency) and the complete security incident management process are defined in our Security Assurance Plan. To report a vulnerability: security@query.vision.
