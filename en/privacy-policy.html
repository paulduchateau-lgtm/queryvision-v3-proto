Preamble

This Privacy policy is addressed to Users of the QueryVision platform (hereinafter the "Platform"), published by QueryVision. It informs you about how we collect and process your personal information.

Respect for privacy is a priority: we undertake to process your data in strict compliance with the French Data Protection Act of January 6, 1978, as amended (hereinafter the "DPA") and the EU General Data Protection Regulation of April 27, 2016 (hereinafter the "GDPR"), following two essential principles:

  • each User remains in control of their personal data;
  • data is processed in a transparent, confidential and secure manner.

In providing the Services, QueryVision acts as "Data controller" within the meaning of the GDPR:

  • QueryVision, S.A.S.
  • 9, place Jean Jaurès, 33000 Bordeaux, France
  • Registered office: Bordeaux
  • dpo@query.vision
  • https://www.queryvision.ai

Article 3. Contact details of our DPO

Our Data Protection Officer (hereinafter "DPO") answers all requests relating to your personal data, including requests to exercise your rights. You can reach them:

  • By email: Paul Duchateau, QueryVision — Data Protection Officer — dpo@query.vision

The DPO acts as the point of contact for data subjects, for the CNIL (the French data protection authority) and for the DPOs of participating processors or controllers.

Article 4. How do we collect your data?

As a rule, all data concerning you is collected directly from you (creation of an Associated Account, exchanges with QueryVision). By exception, we may collect your data from the Client in order to give you access to the Platform as a Beneficiary.

We only collect the data necessary for explicit and specified purposes:

Article 6. Types of data collected

Depending on the processing concerned, we may collect:

  • Information associated with the Account (title, last name, first name, work email address);
  • Connection logs, hardware identification data, Services and API usage data, action history;
  • Data collected via cookies and technical trackers;
  • Contractual and billing information;
  • Payment data (a card's security code is deleted as soon as the transaction is completed);
  • Usage statistics.

Whether data is mandatory or optional is indicated at the time of collection ("*" mark). Failure to provide mandatory data may lead QueryVision to refuse you access to the Services.

Article 7. Who can access your data?

Within the limits of their duties and for the purposes described above, the following may access your data:

  • authorized QueryVision personnel;
  • authorized personnel of processors (Platform hosting provider…);
  • third parties involved in billing (banks, payment providers);
  • where applicable, courts, chartered accountants, statutory auditors, lawyers, police authorities in the event of a legal requisition;
  • third parties that may set commercial cookies where you consent to them.

Your data is neither sold nor rented to any other person without your express prior consent.

We keep your data only for as long as necessary for the purposes pursued:

Article 9. What are your rights?

In accordance with the DPA and the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right to withdraw your consent at any time (article 7 GDPR);
  • Right to define the fate of your data after your death (article 85 of the DPA);
  • Right of access (article 15 GDPR);
  • Right to rectification (article 16 GDPR);
  • Right to erasure (article 17 GDPR);
  • Right to restriction of processing (article 18 GDPR);
  • Right to object (article 21 GDPR);
  • Right to data portability (article 20 GDPR).

These rights may be exercised by contacting our DPO:

  • By email: dpo@query.vision;
  • By post: QueryVision — 9, place Jean Jaurès, 33000 Bordeaux, France.

You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL. To date, QueryVision does not carry out any automated decision-making producing legal effects concerning you.

Article 10. Cookies and other trackers

QueryVision uses connection data and cookies to identify you, remember your visits and produce audience statistics. Depending on the purposes, you can consent to, refuse or choose the setting of certain cookies. Details are provided in our Cookie policy.

Article 11. Is your data transferred outside the EU?

As a rule, we process your data within the European Union. Data is stored at Scaleway S.A.S. — a sovereign hosting provider located within the European Union — and the artificial intelligence models are deployed within the European Union.

As our business evolves, we might carry out transfers outside the European Economic Area. In that case, we would keep you informed.

Article 12. What security measures are in place?

We implement all the technical and organizational measures required to guarantee the security of our processing and the confidentiality of data: physical protection of premises, authentication of persons accessing data via confidential logins and passwords, logging and traceability of connections, encryption and/or anonymization, rigorous access management, incident management procedures, penetration testing and team awareness training.

Service level commitments. QueryVision uses all reasonable efforts to achieve an availability rate of 99.5% / month, excluding planned maintenance or force majeure.
