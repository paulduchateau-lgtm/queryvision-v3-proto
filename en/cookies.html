What is a cookie? Cookies we use Managing your consent Learn more

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website or application. It is used in particular to identify you, remember your visits and produce audience measurements and statistics.

In addition to cookies, we use connection data (date, time, Internet address, visitor's device protocol, page viewed) required for the operation and security of the platform.

2. Cookies we use

Depending on their purpose, you can consent to, refuse or choose the setting of certain types of cookies on your device:

CategoryPurposeLegal basis
Technical cookiesEssential to the proper functioning of the platform and its solutions (authentication, security, session persistence, API control).Legitimate interest — exempt from consent.
Audience measurement cookiesStatistical analysis of platform usage in order to improve its experience and quality.Your consent.
Commercial cookies (third-party)Cookies that may be set by third parties for personalization and prospecting purposes, where you consent to them.Your consent.

3. Managing your consent

On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or refuse the setting of cookies that are not strictly necessary. You can change your choices at any time.

You can also configure your browser to accept or refuse cookies, on a case-by-case basis or systematically:

  • Google Chrome — Settings › Privacy and security › Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox — Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge — Settings › Cookies and site permissions
  • Safari — Settings › Privacy

Refusing technical cookies may degrade, or even prevent, access to certain platform features.

4. Learn more

Details of the purposes, the data processed and the associated retention periods are provided in our Privacy policy. For any question, you can contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@query.vision.
