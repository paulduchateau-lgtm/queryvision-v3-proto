Governed scope

Each team sees its own scope. Nothing more.

Document corpora are attached to workspaces, and access rights mirror your organization. Spot Vision answers on substance, from the entrusted scope — it never promises what it doesn't have.

  • Partitioning by workspace and by access rights
  • Content validated before being made available
  • Administered by your own teams, in ADMIN

02

03

Ask
